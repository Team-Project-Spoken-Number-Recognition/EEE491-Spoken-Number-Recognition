## 3. Deviations and analysis (attempts before the evidence run)
Two earlier sessions ended with `HW_RESULT: FAIL` — raw output in
`docs/verification/2026-09-26_hw_debug_session_attempts.log`:
- **Attempt 2, step 12:** 65 544 extra bytes = exactly one additional complete frame. Eren confirmed he pressed
  BTNU once more **after** LD6 was on again → a legitimate new transfer, correct behaviour.
- **Attempt 1, steps 1–2:** 48 960 extra bytes after step 1 plus 16 584 bytes at the start of step 2 =
  65 544 = one additional complete frame starting within 2 s after step 1 (same cause: an extra press after the
  transfer). Step 2 therefore started reading in the middle of a frame → `Start word is 2FCFD030` — the
  decoder rejected the misaligned stream instead of returning shifted data (REQ-SW-003 working on hardware).
- Why not button bounce: a second transfer needs a new debounced 0 → 1 press **after** the 0.62 s transfer;
  bounce lasts < 10 ms and presses during the transfer are ignored (TB-TOPDBG-02/03, HW-DEBUG-04a/b).
- In no attempt was a frame that was read from its first byte corrupted.

Lesson for the demo procedure: press BTNU only when LD6 is on and the previous transfer is complete.
